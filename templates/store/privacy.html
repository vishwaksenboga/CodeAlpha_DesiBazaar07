Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, make a purchase, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment information.

We use cookies to improve your shopping experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
