OUR MISSION

Why We Started DesiBazaar

India is home to thousands of unique regional products — from Kashmir's precious saffron to Kerala's aromatic spices, from Rajasthan's vibrant handicrafts to West Bengal's world-famous sweets. But these artisans and farmers rarely get the visibility or fair prices they deserve.

DesiBazaar was founded to bridge this gap. We work directly with verified producers, artisans, and organic farmers across all 28 states to bring you genuinely authentic products while ensuring fair trade and sustainable livelihoods for producers.

OUR VALUES

What We Stand For

Fair Trade
We ensure producers get fair prices for their work, with 70% of every sale going directly to the maker.
Authenticity
Every product is verified for authenticity. We do on-site audits and maintain strict quality standards.
Sustainability
Eco-friendly packaging, zero-plastic shipping, and preference for organic and sustainable products.
Community
We invest in artisan training, digital literacy, and market access programs for rural producers.
