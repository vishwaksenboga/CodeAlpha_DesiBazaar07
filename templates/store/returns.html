How to Return

  1. Go to "My Orders" and select the order you want to return
  2. Click "Request Return" and select a reason
  3. Our team will review and approve within 24 hours
  4. We'll schedule a free pickup from your address
  5. Refund is processed within 5-7 business days after pickup

Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable food items (unless damaged or defective)
  • Opened edible products
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Intimate or sanitary goods

Refund Methods

Refunds are credited to the original payment method — UPI, bank account, or card. COD orders are refunded via bank transfer within 7 business days.
