Home›About Us

About DesiBazaar

We are on a mission to connect India's rich artisanal and agricultural heritage with modern consumers across the country and the world.

Our Story

DesiBazaar was founded in 2024 with a simple but powerful idea: what if every Indian could access the most authentic regional products from across the country, without leaving their home? From the saffron fields of Kashmir to the banana farms of Kerala, from the brass workshops of Rajasthan to the weaving centers of West Bengal — India's diversity is its greatest treasure.

We started by partnering with 50 artisans and farmers. Today, we work with thousands of makers across 18+ Indian states, offering 400+ products that tell the story of India's incredible culture and craftsmanship.

Our Mission

To empower Indian artisans, farmers, and small businesses by giving them a platform to reach customers nationwide. We believe authentic Indian products deserve premium positioning and fair pricing — and that consumers deserve access to the real thing.

Our Values

Authenticity — Every product is verified for regional origin and genuine craftsmanship.
Sustainability — We use eco-friendly packaging and support sustainable farming practices.
Fair Trade — We ensure artisans and farmers receive fair compensation for their work.
Community — We invest in the communities we source from.

Returns & Refunds

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

30-Day Return Policy

You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Products must be returned in their original condition and packaging.

How to Return

1. Go to My Orders and select the order you want to return.
2. Click "Return Item" and select the reason for return.
3. Schedule a free pickup or drop off at the nearest courier center.
4. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

Non-Returnable Items

Perishable goods (food items), personalized products, and items marked as non-returnable at the time of purchase cannot be returned.

Refund Process

Refunds are processed to the original payment method. UPI/card refunds take 3-5 business days. Bank transfers take 5-7 business days.

Damaged Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, contact us within 48 hours with photos at returns@desibazaar.in for an immediate replacement or refund.
